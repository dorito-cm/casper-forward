## Why Casper Forward

**Section label:** WHY CASPER FORWARD

**Headline:** Hackathons should be a start, not an ending.

A working prototype is only the beginning. Technical blockers, product decisions, infrastructure needs and launch preparation can all slow promising teams down after an event ends. Casper Forward keeps serious builders connected while they work through what comes next.

### 01 / CONTINUE

**Headline:** Keep the momentum.

Stay connected to Casper beyond the event and continue building around your own product timeline.

### 02 / UNBLOCK

**Headline:** Clear the next obstacle.

Surface technical, infrastructure, product or GTM blockers and connect with the right support.

### 03 / LAUNCH

**Headline:** Move toward production.

Turn a promising build into something ready for Mainnet, users and continued development.

## Builder Journey

**Section label:** BUILDER JOURNEY

**Headline:** A clear path forward.

Every project is different. Progress is based on readiness and the next useful milestone — not a fixed cohort schedule.

### 01 Apply

Tell us what you’ve built, where the project stands and what you need next.

### 02 Align

Define the next milestone, current blockers and a realistic path forward.

### 03 Build

Keep developing with access to relevant technical, ecosystem and GTM support.

### 04 Prepare

Work through security, infrastructure and Mainnet readiness.

### 05 Launch & Grow

Deploy when ready, reach users and stay connected as the product evolves.

## Eligibility

**Section label:** ELIGIBILITY

**Headline:** For teams that intend to keep going.

Casper Forward is designed for projects showing meaningful progress and a clear intention to continue building on Casper.

### Who is it for?

- Buildathon finalists.
- Strong Buildathon teams continuing development.
- Previous Casper hackathon teams still actively building.
- Other builders with a functional Casper Testnet MVP.

### What matters?

- Meaningful technical progress.
- Clear intent to continue building on Casper.
- A realistic next milestone.
- Active engagement with the project.
- Open-source readiness.
