## Eligibility

**Section label:** ELIGIBILITY

**Headline:** For selected teams ready to take their projects further.

Casper Forward is designed for projects that have demonstrated meaningful progress and have a clear plan to continue developing on Casper.

### Who is it for?

- Buildathon finalists.
- Strong Buildathon teams continuing development.
- Previous Casper hackathon teams still actively building.
- Other builders with a functional Casper Testnet MVP.

### What matters?

- Meaningful technical progress.
- Clear intent to continue building on Casper.
- A realistic next milestone.
- Active engagement with the project.
- Open-source readiness.
